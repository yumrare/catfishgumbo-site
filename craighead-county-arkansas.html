Craighead County, Arkansas

18,473 Black residents · 16.6% of the county · ranked 4 of 75 in Arkansas by Black population

Craighead County is a Arkansas county entry in the gazetteer's first edition. In the 2020 Census, Craighead County held 111,231 residents in all, of whom 18,473 — 16.6% — were Black alone, the count this gazetteer records for every place on the same terms.

Population Trend

The two-census record for Craighead County: 12,640 Black residents in 2010 and 18,473 in 2020. The population grew by 46.1%, and the Black share in Craighead County moved from 13.1% to 16.6%.
