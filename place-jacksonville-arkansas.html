Jacksonville, Arkansas

11,876 Black residents · 40.3% of the place

Population Trend

The published record across two censuses for Jacksonville: 9,272 Black residents in 2010, 11,876 in 2020 — grew by 28.1% over the decade, with the Black share of the whole moving from 32.7% to 40.3%.
